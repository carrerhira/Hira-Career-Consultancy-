FAQ

Questions you may have

What is the registration fee?

Registration starts at ₹20,000 per contract. The exact amount depends on your requirements and chosen package, and is confirmed in the agreement.

How many roles are covered?

Your designated role requirements are covered under one annual agreement. Role limits are defined in the signed contract.

Do you handle fresher hiring too?

Yes. We support both fresher and experienced hiring, including bulk requirements.

Which locations do you cover?

We hire across agreed roles and locations in India. Share your location requirements and we will confirm coverage.

How soon will I receive shortlists?

Turnaround time depends on the role and volume, and is confirmed when the agreement is signed.

Are there any extra charges?

Any additional charges are clearly explained before you sign. There are no hidden charges.
